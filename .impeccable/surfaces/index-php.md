# Surface brief: whole shop (all pages)

Scope: Persuade on the catalogue and book pages, Operate on cart, orders, staff desk. Visitor: Thai students and general readers, mostly on phones. Action: find a book, see rent plus refundable deposit, order, track the parcel.
Chosen direction: the Thai book fair hall (a book fair floor in full swing). Unresolved: real photos of the shop do not exist and must not be invented.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The shop is a book fair hall you can rent from: coloured zones, price flags and hand-written price cards, not a template storefront with a cream paper skin. It refuses the clean white grid of identical cards under a hero.
OWN-WORLD: Hall-blue ink #12206e on a cool white floor, banner yellow #ffd21f, flag red #e5322d, and five zone colours (fantasy violet, science green, history orange, literature rose, computing cyan) that tint category tags, covers' backing board and zone headings. Components are fair objects: bunting strip of flags, booth fascia signs with a zone letter, price tag with a punched hole carrying baht per day, starburst sticker for sold-out and new, a hall floor map as category navigation, ticket-stub order timeline. Display face Prompt 700-800 for banners and prices, Sarabun for reading. Dark mode is the hall after closing: deep navy floor, same zone colours, yellow stays.
STORY: A first-time visitor sees the fair is open, understands rent from a few baht a day with a refundable deposit, picks a zone on the floor map or searches, and reaches a book with the true total shown; after ordering the same visitor always sees where the parcel is.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Bunting strip across the top edge, banner headline with rent price line and a search field built as a fair information desk, beneath it the hall floor map: five coloured zone blocks with book counts acting as category buttons, then the first row of price-tagged covers peeking above the fold. Primary action is the search/zone choice, then add to cart on any tag.
